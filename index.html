Get a natural-sounding voice (free, one time)

Your phone and laptop already include free, human-sounding voices. They just need a one-time download. Once you have one, it appears at the top of the voice list here.

iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → pick Ava, Zoe or Evan → download the Premium version.

Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System voice → Manage Voices → English → tick Ava (Premium) or Zoe (Premium). Then reload this page.

Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech output → Google → Install voice data → English → pick a voice.

Free. Your articles and progress stay on this device.
