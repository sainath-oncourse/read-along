About the voices

The voices are free, open-source AI voices that run on your own phone or laptop. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is charged.

Studio voices sound the most human. They need a newer browser (Chrome on a laptop, or Safari on iOS 26) and a one-time 330 MB download, so use Wi-Fi the first time.

Light voices work on any phone. Each is a one-time download of about 60 MB.

After the first download, the voice starts instantly and works offline. Change voice from the Voice button while listening.
